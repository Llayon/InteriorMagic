## Evidence quality notes

- **301/307/401 are not "no evidence"** — they confirm the host is official but gated; secondary mirrors (DragonSigh for Ozon, salacoste for WB, selsup/1clancer for Lemana/Hoff) are used only to interpret structure, never to invent an official capability.
- **Affiliate commissions quoting secondary sources** (Pampadu/Partnerkin/Admitad) are corroborating, not primary. Primary would be the signed affiliate contract (not fetched). Rates are presented as ranges with caveats.
- **Yandex Market affiliate legal doc S37 is the only fetched terms page that enumerates data-rights constraints** — hence Market is the only retailer where display-with-attribution is ALLOWED and where cache/proxy/embedding rights are explicitly UNKNOWN (require confirmation) rather than wholly UNKNOWN.
- **All product-image embedding conclusions** are per correction: prefer UNKNOWN / REQUIRES COMMERCIAL/LEGAL CONFIRMATION unless official terms materially support the conclusion. No retailer met that bar in this window.
